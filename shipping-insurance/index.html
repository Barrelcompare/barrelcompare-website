Barrel shipping insurance

What's covered, what isn't, and how to protect your goods in transit from the UK to the Caribbean.

Are your barrel contents insured?

In most cases, barrel contents are not automatically insured when shipped from the UK to the Caribbean. Most shipping companies operate on a limited liability basis — meaning if goods are lost or damaged, you may only receive a small fixed payout, not the replacement value of your items.

What typically isn't covered

  • Electronics, appliances, and high-value items
  • Breakage or damage during loading and transit
  • Loss due to customs seizure of restricted items
  • Theft at the destination port
  • Spoilage of perishable goods

How to protect yourself

  • Ask your chosen supplier/agent directly whether they offer transit insurance and at what cost
  • Declare the approximate value of your barrel's contents when booking
  • Keep a written list of items packed and their estimated value
  • Photograph the contents before sealing the barrel
  • Check whether your home contents insurance covers goods in transit
