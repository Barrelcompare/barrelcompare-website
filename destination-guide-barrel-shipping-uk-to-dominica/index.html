🇩🇲 Barrel Shipping UK to Dominica

Dominica handles a high volume of family barrel shipments from the UK, with relatively straightforward clearance for used household goods. Agricultural restrictions are an important consideration when packing.

Customs & Duties — What to Expect

Dominica customs apply relatively consistent rules for personal barrel shipments, with particular attention on food and plant-based items. Import controls on restricted goods are enforced.

  • Inspection of food and plant-based items
  • Potential seizure of restricted agricultural goods
  • Duties applied to electronics and new appliances
  • Delays linked to incomplete paperwork or unclear declarations

Key Packing Advice

Proper item labelling and separation of food items reduces clearance risk significantly. Avoid packing restricted agricultural or plant-based goods, and ensure all items are accurately declared on your packing list.
